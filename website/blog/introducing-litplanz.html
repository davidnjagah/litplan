Introducing LitPlanz: Your AI Guide to Every City

Every great night out starts with the same question: where should we go? LitPlanz exists to answer it in seconds, wherever you are.

Why we built LitPlanz

Finding something great to do in a city shouldn't mean bouncing between review sites, map apps, social feeds, and a group chat that never decides. We wanted one place that understands what you're in the mood for and shows you experiences that actually fit.

So we built LitPlanz: an AI-powered guide that curates the best restaurants, bars, events, attractions, and hidden gems across 100+ cities worldwide, from New York and Miami to London, Tokyo, and Dubai.

What you can do with LitPlanz

  • Explore cities. Browse curated experiences in your city or wherever you're headed next.
  • Get AI recommendations. LitPlanz learns your preferences and suggests places that match your taste.
  • Use Vibez Mode. Pick a mood, whether it's a relaxed afternoon or an adventurous evening, and get instant matches.
  • Build Group Flows. Plan multi-stop itineraries for your crew, from brunch to sightseeing to dinner.
  • Save & share. Keep your favorite spots and send your plans to friends right from the app.

Built for locals and travelers

Whether you've lived somewhere for ten years or just landed, there's always something you haven't tried. LitPlanz helps locals break out of their usual rotation and helps travelers skip the tourist traps.

Our goal is simple: less time deciding, more time living it.

What's next

We're shipping improvements constantly, and this blog is where we'll share city guides, planning tips, and product updates. Have an idea or feedback? Email us at support@litplanz.com. We read everything.
