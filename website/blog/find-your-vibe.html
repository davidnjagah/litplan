Find Your Vibe: Why Mood Beats Category When Choosing Where to Go

"Restaurants near me" returns hundreds of results. "Something chill and cozy tonight" is what you actually meant. That gap is why we built Vibez Mode.

The problem with categories

Most discovery apps make you start with a category: restaurants, bars, museums, events. But that's rarely how people decide. You don't wake up wanting "a restaurant." You want a lazy brunch with good coffee, or a loud, high-energy night, or somewhere quiet to actually talk.

Two places in the same category can feel completely different. A candlelit wine bar and a packed sports bar are both "bars," but they're not interchangeable at all.

Start with how you want to feel

Vibez Mode flips the process. Instead of picking a category first, you pick a mood, and LitPlanz finds experiences across every category that match it. A "relaxed" vibe might surface a rooftop café, a botanical garden, and a jazz lounge, all in one go.

A few ways people use it

  • Date night: set a romantic or low-key vibe and skip the places that are too loud to hear each other.
  • New in town: choose an adventurous vibe to find the spots locals love but tourists miss.
  • Sunday reset: go for something calm: a long walk, a slow brunch, a bookshop.
  • Celebration: turn the energy up and find somewhere that matches the occasion.

It gets better the more you use it

LitPlanz's AI learns from what you save and explore, so your recommendations get more personal over time. The more you use Vibez Mode, the better it gets at knowing what "chill" means to you.
