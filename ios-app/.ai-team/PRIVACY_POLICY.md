## Introduction

Litplan ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Litplan mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.

**By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.**

If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account or use the App, we collect the following personal information:

**Firebase Authentication Data:**
- Email address
- Display name (optional)
- Profile photo (optional, via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In)
- User ID (unique identifier)

**User-Generated Content:**
- Saved cities (cities you've viewed or favorited)
- Plan interaction data (which plans you've viewed)
- Preferences and settings

**Subscription Information:**
- Subscription status (Free or Pro)
- Subscription type (Monthly or Yearly)
- Transaction IDs from Apple (for subscription verification)
- Subscription renewal dates
- Purchase receipts (validated via Apple's servers)

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information:

**Usage Analytics:**
- App interactions (screen views, button taps, feature usage)
- Session duration and frequency
- Device type and operating system version
- App version
- Crash reports and error logs
- Performance metrics (load times, API response times)

**Location Data:**
- We do NOT collect your precise location
- We do NOT track your physical movements
- City selection is manual and user-initiated

**Device Information:**
- Device model (iPhone, iPad)
- Operating system (iOS version)
- Screen size and resolution
- Language and region settings
- Network connection type (WiFi, cellular)

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to minimal data collection:

- We do NOT collect your precise location or GPS coordinates
- We do NOT collect your contacts or address book
- We do NOT collect your photos (except profile photo if you choose to add one)
- We do NOT collect your browsing history outside the App
- We do NOT collect sensitive personal information (race, religion, health data)
- We do NOT use facial recognition or biometric data
- We do NOT track your physical visits to venues or locations

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

### 1. Provide and Maintain the App
- Create and manage your user account
- Authenticate your identity (via Firebase Authentication)
- Display personalized nightlife plans and recommendations
- Remember your viewed cities and preferences
- Enable search and filtering functionality

### 2. Process Subscriptions
- Validate subscription purchases via Apple
- Grant access to Pro features (unlimited cities, 50 plans per city, weekend plans)
- Manage subscription renewals and cancellations
- Process refunds and restore purchases

### 3. Improve the App
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
- Measure app performance and load times
- Test new features with analytics data
- Optimize user experience and interface design

### 4. Communicate with You
- Send transactional emails (subscription confirmations, receipts)
- Respond to support requests and inquiries
- Notify you of app updates or policy changes (with your consent)
- Send promotional messages (only with explicit opt-in consent)

### 5. Ensure Security and Compliance
- Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect the rights and safety of our users

## How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and limit data sharing to essential service providers:

### 1. Service Providers

**Firebase (Google Cloud Platform):**
- Purpose: User authentication, database storage, analytics
- Data Shared: Email, display name, user ID, app usage data
- Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Location: United States (data stored in US Firebase servers)

**Apple Inc.:**
- Purpose: Subscription management, payment processing
- Data Shared: Transaction IDs, subscription status, purchase receipts
- Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Location: United States

**Anthropic (Claude API):**
- Purpose: AI-powered nightlife plan generation
- Data Shared: City names ONLY (no personal information)
- Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- Location: United States
- Note: We do NOT share user names, emails, or any personal data with Anthropic

### 2. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:
- Valid legal processes (court orders, subpoenas, search warrants)
- Government investigations or requests
- Threats to public safety or national security
- Protection of our legal rights or property

### 3. Business Transfers

If Litplan is acquired by or merged with another company, your information may be transferred to the new owner. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice in the App before your information is transferred.

### 4. What We Do NOT Share

- We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties
- We do NOT share your data with advertisers or data brokers
- We do NOT allow third-party advertising networks to track you
- We do NOT share your data for marketing purposes (except our own)

## Data Retention

### Active Accounts
- We retain your account data indefinitely while your account is active
- You can delete your account at any time (see "Your Rights" below)

### Deleted Accounts
- When you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal information within 30 days
- Backup copies are deleted within 90 days
- We may retain anonymized analytics data for historical analysis

### Subscription Data
- Subscription transaction records are retained for 7 years (required for tax compliance)
- Transaction records are anonymized after account deletion

### Legal Holds
- If required by law, we may retain certain data beyond normal retention periods

## Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 1. Access Your Data
- View your account information in the App (Profile tab)
- Request a copy of your data by emailing support@litplan.app
- Receive your data in a portable format (JSON or CSV)

### 2. Correct Your Data
- Update your display name and profile photo in the App
- Correct inaccurate information by contacting support

### 3. Delete Your Data
- Delete your account in the App (Profile → Settings → Delete Account)
- Request deletion by emailing support@litplan.app
- All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

### 4. Opt-Out of Analytics
- Disable analytics in App settings (coming soon)
- Email support@litplan.app to opt-out manually

### 5. Manage Subscription
- Cancel subscription in App Store settings (not in the App)
- Request refund via Apple Support
- Restore purchases in the App (Profile → Restore Purchases)

### 6. Data Portability
- Request export of your data (cities viewed, plan interactions)
- Receive data in machine-readable format

### 7. Withdraw Consent
- Opt-out of promotional emails (unsubscribe link in emails)
- Revoke account permissions (delete account)

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

### Encryption
- **In Transit:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **At Rest:** Firebase database uses AES-256 encryption for data storage
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect

### Access Controls
- Strict access controls limit who can view your data
- Only authorized personnel can access production databases
- Multi-factor authentication required for admin access

### Security Monitoring
- Continuous monitoring for security threats
- Automated alerts for suspicious activity
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

### Limitations
- No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure
- We cannot guarantee absolute security
- You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials
