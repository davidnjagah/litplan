---

Life is a combination of magic and LitPlanz. The best nights don't just happen — they start with the right plan, the right vibe, and the right crew. Don't settle for another boring night or a last-minute scramble. Add LitPlanz to the mix and let the city do the rest.

**LitPlanz** is your AI-powered nightlife co-pilot. Tell it your vibe, pick your city, and watch it build a full night out in seconds — real venues, curated routes, and hand-picked experiences that actually match your energy.

Whether you're planning a big group night, a date, a solo adventure, or a spontaneous Thursday, LitPlanz does the heavy lifting so you can focus on showing up and having a great time.

**What LitPlanz does for you:**

- **Vibez** — generate a complete multi-stop night out in one tap. Bars, clubs, restaurants, jazz rooms, rooftops — wherever the night takes you, we've mapped it out
- **Route Maps** — see your full night plotted on a map and open turn-by-turn directions straight into Apple Maps
- **Save your favourites** — bookmark the cities, plans, and Vibez you love so your next night out is always one tap away
- **Group-ready** — built for squads. Every plan comes with group cost estimates, duration, and difficulty so the whole crew is on the same page

**Free or Pro — your call:**

Free users get one Vibez per week to try it out and save up to 3 plans, cities, and Vibez. Go Pro for 7 Vibez per week, unlimited saves, your full Generated Vibez history, and priority access to new cities and features.
