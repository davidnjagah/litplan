# What's New — LitPlanz v2.3

---

**Pro subscriptions show correctly again**

Fixed an issue where some users with an active subscription were occasionally seen as Free. Pro is now correctly recognised on every sign-in and stays bound to your account — so on a shared phone, your Pro doesn't follow another account.

**Polished login layout**

The Sign in with Apple button now matches Sign in with Google and Continue with Email in width and shape — perfectly aligned on every iPhone size.

**Bug fixes and stability improvements**

General performance and reliability improvements throughout.
